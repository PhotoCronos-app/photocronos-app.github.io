PhotoCronos Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

PhotoCronos is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. The application operates locally on your device and does not require an account, registration, or internet connection.

2. Data Collection

PhotoCronos does not collect, transmit, or share personal data with the developer or any external service. We do not operate servers or cloud storage for the application.

3. Photos and Media

Photos and videos accessed by PhotoCronos remain on your device. Media is processed locally for the application's functionality. Your media is not uploaded to the developer or transmitted to third parties.

4. Tracking and Analytics

PhotoCronos does not use advertising trackers, analytics services, or third-party data collection services.

5. Data Storage and Security

PhotoCronos does not maintain a remote database containing user data. Information handled by the application remains under the user's control on their own device.

6. Children's Privacy

PhotoCronos does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be available at this page.
